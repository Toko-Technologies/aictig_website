# Updating the AICTiG website: dashboard guide

The dashboard lets you change the website yourself, without a developer.
Changes go live as soon as you click **Save**.

**Sign in:** go to **aictig.org/admin** and use the email and password you were given.

---

## What you can change

| In the menu | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **News** | News articles on the News page and the home page. |
| **Publications** | Articles, policy briefs and reports on the Publications page. |
| **Repository** | The laws, policies and strategies in the policy repository: upload documents and describe them. |
| **Website pages** | All the text on the Home, About, Focus Areas, Consultancy and Contact pages, plus the top menu and the footer (including office addresses). |
| **Messages** | Messages visitors send through the contact form on the website. A number next to “Messages” shows how many are unread. |
| **Team members** | The experts on the About page: photo, name, role, biography, areas of expertise, projects, publications and conferences. |
| **Contact details** | The email addresses in the footer and the social media icons (Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, WhatsApp). |
| **Translation** | Automatic translation into the other languages: switch it on or off, protected words, and how much of today's free allowance is used. |
| **Change history** | A list of every change: who made it, when, and the text before and after. |
| **Dashboard users** | Who can sign in to the dashboard. |
| **My account** | Change your password. |

**Older news, publications and documents** that were published through WordPress
stay on the website exactly as before. In the dashboard they are marked
**From WordPress** and cannot be changed here. Everything you add from now on is
added in the dashboard.

## News and publications

1. Open **News** (or **Publications**) and click **Add news article**.
2. Fill in the **Title**, a short **Summary** and the **Text**. The toolbar above
   the text has headings, bold, italic, lists, quotes, links, pictures and file
   attachments (PDF or Word).
3. Add a **Picture** (shown at the top and on the article card), the **Date** and,
   if you like, **Tags**: items with the same tag are shown as related.
   Publications also have a **type**, **authors** and an optional **PDF to download**.
4. Click **Publish** to put it on the website, or **Save as draft** to finish
   it later. Drafts are not shown on the website.

To take something off the website for now, open it and click **Unpublish**.
**Delete** removes it completely.

## Repository documents

1. Open **Repository** and click **Add document**.
2. Choose the file (PDF or Word, up to 30 MB). The title is filled in from the
   file name: correct it to the official name.
3. Choose the **year**, then either **one country** or **a region or organisation**
   (for example ECOWAS or the African Union), the **languages** and the
   **themes**. Visitors use these to find documents.
4. Click **Add to repository**.

To change a document, click **Edit**: you can correct its details or replace the
file. Turn off **Show on the website** to hide it without deleting it.

## Changing text on a page

1. Open **Website pages** and choose the page (for example **Home page**).
2. Each box is one piece of text on the page, grouped as it appears on the
   website (for example **Top banner**). The small grey note under a box says
   where it appears.
3. Type your new text. A yellow **Not saved** label appears, and a bar at the
   bottom shows how many changes are waiting.
4. Click **Save changes**. Then click **View page** to see the result.

- **Edited** (green) means the text differs from the original website text.
- **Restore original** puts the original wording back (then click **Save changes**).
- **Discard** throws away the changes you have not saved yet.
- On long pages, use **Find a text…** or the **Sections** list on the left.

### Languages

The website is available in 7 languages. Use the language menu at the top of
the page to choose which version you are editing.

**Automatic translation** (on unless someone switched it off under **Translation**):

- Change the **English** text and save. A few seconds later the other 6
  languages are updated automatically. Those texts show a blue
  **Auto-translated** label.
- Want different wording in, say, French? Edit it on the French version. Your
  wording is kept and is no longer updated automatically.
- **Restore original** on the English text puts every language back to its
  original wording.

- Machine translation is good but not perfect: check important headings in
  the other languages.
- Names that must stay as they are (for example TETRALEX or people's names) can
  be added under **Translation → Words that must never be translated**.
  "AICTiG", {{year}}, web addresses and email addresses are always kept.
- Office addresses are copied as they are, not translated.
- The free service translates about 5,000 characters a day (50,000 if a contact
  email is entered on the Translation page). Anything left over is translated
  automatically the next day.

When translation is **off**, each language is edited separately.

## Messages

Visitors write to you through the form on the **Contact** page. Open
**Messages** to read them. Opening a message marks it as read.

- **Reply by email** opens your email program with the visitor's address and a
  subject already filled in.
- **Mark as unread** if you want to come back to it later.
- **Delete** removes a message permanently.

Spam is filtered automatically, and each visitor can send at most 5 messages an hour.

---

## Team members

- **Add team member** creates a new profile. Name, role, short summary and
  biography are required.
- **Upload photo** accepts JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB. A portrait (taller than
  wide) looks best.
- **Show on the website** off hides someone without deleting their profile.
- Use the arrows next to each person to change the order on the About page.
- **Lists** (expertise, projects, publications, conferences): one item per line.
- **Biography:** leave an empty line between paragraphs.
- **Links:** write `[text to show](https://web-address)`. **Italics:** `*like this*`.
  Click **Preview** to see how it will look before saving.

---

## Good to know

- You are signed out after 2 hours without activity, or after 8 hours in total.
- After 5 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
- **Forgot your password?** Ask another dashboard user to remove and re-add
  you, or ask the website administrator to reset it.
- Made a mistake? Open **Change history**, click **Show details**, and copy the
  previous wording back into the page.
